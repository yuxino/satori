# Satori identity design

## Direction

Satori belongs to the same visual family as Mimi and Kiri: a warm ivory rounded-square macOS icon, refined monochrome anime line art, a white-haired original character, pale lavender accents, and one small product-specific symbol.

The product-specific moment is understanding. The character is reading an open book while a restrained warm-gold spark rises from the pages. The book remains readable at small sizes, the spark separates Satori from the lavender-only sibling icons, and the composition avoids generic PDF, robot, or chat imagery.

## Production assets

- `Resources/Assets/satori-icon.png`: 1024 × 1024 RGBA source used by the README
- `Resources/satori.icns`: packaged macOS application icon
- `Resources/Info.plist`: declares `satori.icns` as the bundle icon

## Guardrails

- No wordmark inside the icon
- No copied Kiri or Mimi pose
- No photorealism or saturated palette
- The book and insight spark must remain legible at Dock size
- Future UI colors may reuse lavender and warm gold, but the icon should not force a decorative anime theme into the reading surface
